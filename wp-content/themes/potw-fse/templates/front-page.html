About People of the Word

People of the Word is a Bible teaching ministry dedicated to helping people grow in grace and knowledge through the methodical study of Scripture.

For over 50 years, we've provided teaching, guided lessons, and a community where people of all ages can learn, discuss, and grow together.

The Study is for…

Those who want a deeper understanding of the Bible

People looking for structured study

Anyone seeking spiritual growth through Christ
